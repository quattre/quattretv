Quattre Internet S.L. is a Spanish telecommunications operator based in
Rafelbunyol, in the province of Valencia. We build and run our own fibre optic
network and provide internet access, telephony and television directly to
homes and businesses in the region.

Our television service is delivered over our own managed network, from our own
head-end and content delivery servers located in Spain. Subscribers watch it on
the set-top box we install, and we are now bringing the same service to the
televisions they already own, so that a customer does not need an extra box in
every room.

The QuattreTV application is that television service: the channel line-up the
customer has contracted, the programme guide, and nothing else. It is free to
install, contains no advertising and no purchases; the service itself is
contracted with us, as their telecommunications provider.

We are not a content aggregator or a streaming platform. We are the operator
that brings the line the customer pays for, and the application is simply how
that service reaches the screen.
