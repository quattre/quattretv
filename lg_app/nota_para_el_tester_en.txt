This is the first submission of this app.

Desired platform: webOS 3.0 and later (2016 and later models). The app is written
in ES5 with prefixed flexbox only, and has been verified on a webOS 4.5 set
(model 32LM6380PLC). It is a webOS app and does not target Netcast.

Desired launch date: as soon as the review is approved. There is no marketing
date to meet.

NOTES FOR THE TESTER

1. A subscription account is required. The test account is provided in the Test
   Account field. There is no sign-up inside the TV: accounts are created by the
   operator when the customer contracts the service.

2. The app contains no adult material. Our line-up does include one channel
   rated for adults, and it is excluded from the webOS line-up on the server, so
   it is not reachable from this app at all.

3. Channels 1001 to 1016 are radio stations. They carry audio only and have no
   video track. This is expected and is not a playback fault.

4. All content is live television. There is no pause, rewind, or on-demand
   content, so playback controls do not apply.

5. Bandwidth. This is operator IPTV delivered over our own managed fibre
   network, where bandwidth is guaranteed. Channels are broadcast quality at a
   single bitrate, measured between 3.0 and 9.4 Mbps (4.3 Mbps on average), and
   there is no adaptive bitrate ladder. Comfortable playback of any channel
   needs about 12 Mbps; the heaviest channel is number 24. Below that it will
   buffer. This is by design and matches how the service is sold.

6. Service language: Spanish. Service country: Spain.

If anything fails during the review, please write to info@quattre.com and we
will answer the same working day.
